03 / The founder thesis

Question what’s normal.
Build what should exist.

I believe the best opportunities are hiding inside work, industries, and business models everyone else has accepted as normal.

AI is the enabling mechanism of this moment. It gives us the ability to step back and ask:

Why does this exist?
Why do we still do it this way?
Who decided this is how the industry has to work?

  • Why can’t a SaaS company feel as intuitive as Apple?
  • Why can’t enterprise software feel calm and considered like Loro Piana?
  • Why can’t technology make you feel enabled the way Nike does?

My approach is to question the workflow, the business model, and the experience—then use AI and software to build something better.

And sometimes the best answer isn’t to improve the work.

It’s to make the work disappear.
